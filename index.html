Pulse (نبض)

Arabic Middle East news page

Pulse is an Arabic news page covering the Middle East. This site documents the small, private publishing tool, also called Pulse, that the page's operator uses to publish Pulse's own news videos.

The tool is used only by the Pulse team. It uploads videos that Pulse produces to Pulse's own YouTube channel, through Google's official YouTube Data API. It has no public users and offers no sign-in to anyone else.
