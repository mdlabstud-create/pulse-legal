Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 (updated October 27, 2026: added the comment permission)

This policy covers the Pulse publishing tool (the "App"), which the Pulse (نبض) news page uses to publish its own content.

Who uses the App

Only the Pulse team, to upload Pulse's own news videos to Pulse's own YouTube channel. The App has no public users.

Google user data the App accesses

The App asks for two Google permissions, granted by the owner of the Pulse YouTube channel:

  • YouTube upload (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload): to upload Pulse's own news videos, with their titles and descriptions, to Pulse's own channel.
  • YouTube manage (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl): used only to post one comment, on Pulse's own videos, listing the news sources for that video.

The App does not read, collect or store any data about viewers, commenters or any other Google users.

How the data is stored and protected

The authorization token Google issues is stored only on the Pulse operator's own server and is used only to upload Pulse's videos. It is never sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising.

Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
